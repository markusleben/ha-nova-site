HA NOVA — The demo house

A simulated Home Assistant house with no real devices, for trying Home Assistant and HA NOVA before you connect your own.

Before you open the demo

Public demo — everyone shares one account. Anything you enter is visible to other visitors until the next reset. Never enter real names, addresses, passwords, API keys or tokens, and don’t connect real accounts or devices.
