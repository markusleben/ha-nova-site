Privacy

Controller and contact

The controller is the HA NOVA maintainer. Contact: GitHub Issues. Issues are public, so do not put personal data in one; say what you need, and the maintainer will arrange a private channel.

Hosting of hanova.app (GitHub Pages)

hanova.app is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open it, GitHub processes the data needed to deliver it, in particular your IP address, the date and time, and the page requested, and by its own account stores the IP address for security purposes. The purpose is delivering the site securely; the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure, working website). The maintainer has no access to these logs; GitHub decides how long it keeps them.

This transfers data to the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission (Art. 45 GDPR). Details: GitHub's privacy statement.

Cookies and tracking

hanova.app sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics. No fonts, scripts, or images load from third parties.

Demo house (demo.hanova.app)

The public demo house runs on a server at Amazon Web Services (AWS) in Frankfurt am Main, which AWS operates as a processor (Art. 28 GDPR). When you open the demo house or the page hanova.app/demo/ (it asks the demo for its running Home Assistant version), an access log is kept: your shortened IP address (last byte set to 0), the time, the address requested, and technical connection data, no headers. A new log file starts every day at 00:00 UTC; no entry is older than 6 days, and older files are deleted. The purpose is preventing abuse; the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Home Assistant receives only the shortened IP address (last byte set to 0): it is stored in the sign-in token and shown in a notice after failed sign-ins. Both are visible to other visitors, and so is everything you enter in the demo house. The purpose is providing the demo; the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. All of this is wiped at the top of every hour. Do not enter personal data there.

Your browser loads integration icons directly from brands.home-assistant.io, a service of the Open Home Foundation (Switzerland) delivered through Cloudflare. That service receives your IP address; no referrer is sent. The purpose is showing the icons; the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Install count of older versions

Older versions of HA NOVA could, after explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), send a weekly install count: a random install ID (stored only as a hash), the HA NOVA and Relay versions, and the operating system; the time of the first and last report is stored as well. Cloudflare, Inc. (USA, certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) processes your IP address as a processor; the count itself does not store it. Current versions send nothing; updating ends the reports. Entries without a new report are deleted after 60 days. Details: PRIVACY.md.

Obligation to provide data

You are not required by law or contract to provide any data; without an IP address, however, the site and the demo cannot be delivered.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), and restriction of processing (Art. 18). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). To do so, use the contact above. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the member state of your habitual residence, your place of work, or the alleged infringement.

Right to object

You can object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21 GDPR).
